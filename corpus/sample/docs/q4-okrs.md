# Q4 2026 OKRs - Platform & Forecasting

## Objective 1: Cut perishable waste with better forecasts

- KR1: Reduce spoilage by **12%** in stores using Kestrel (baseline: Q3 2026 spoilage).
- KR2: Kestrel live in all **42** stores by November 18.
- KR3: Perishables MAPE below 18% every week of the quarter.

## Objective 2: A faster, sturdier checkout

- KR1: Complete Harbor cutover with zero data loss. (Done Oct 4.)
- KR2: Checkout p95 latency below 250 ms. (Done: 205 ms after Harbor.)
- KR3: No SEV1 incidents caused by the platform in Q4.

## Objective 3: Pantry v3 everyone loves

- KR1: Ship v3.0 to 100% of customers. (Done Oct 21.)
- KR2: Crash-free sessions at least 99.5%.
- KR3: Median repeat-customer time to checkout below 3 minutes (smart reorder).
