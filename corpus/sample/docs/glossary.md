# Glossary

- **SKU**: stock keeping unit, one sellable product (for example, "1 L oat milk, brand X").
- **Store**: one of our dark stores (small warehouses). We have 42 stores across Oregon and Washington.
- **Spoilage**: perishable stock thrown away because it expired. Forecasting exists mainly to reduce it.
- **MAPE**: mean absolute percentage error. Our main forecast-accuracy metric; lower is better.
- **Horizon**: how many days ahead a forecast predicts. Kestrel forecasts up to 14 days ahead.
- **Dark launch**: shipping code behind a flag that is off for everyone, to test in production safely.
- **CDC**: change data capture; streaming every row change out of a database.
- **Cutover**: the moment traffic moves from an old system to a new one.
- **Go/no-go**: the meeting where we decide whether a launch or cutover happens as planned.
- **Smart reorder**: the Pantry app feature that suggests a basket based on past orders and Kestrel forecasts.
