# Customer feedback summary - Q3 2026

Sources: 1,240 support tickets, 380 app store reviews, 12 customer interviews.

## Top themes

1. **Out-of-stock items after ordering** (31% of tickets). Customers order, then get a substitution
   call. Kestrel should help by keeping shelves stocked; smart reorder filters to in-stock items.
2. **Slow checkout on older Android phones** (18%). Pantry v3 performance work addresses this.
3. **Delivery windows fill up too fast** on Sunday mornings (14%). Not our team; passed to Logistics.
4. **Rebuilding the same basket every week** (9%). Smart reorder addresses this directly.

## Quotes

- "I order the same 20 things every week. Why do I have to find them again?"
- "Half my produce got substituted last Tuesday."

## Recommendation

Prioritise smart reorder to 50% of users once the error rate holds below 1%.
