# Q4 planning check-in (October 28)

## Kestrel Oregon expansion

Marcus proposed expanding from 5 pilot stores to all 18 Oregon stores on November 4, two weeks before
the full 42-store rollout.

- Pro: catches problems with a smaller blast radius than going straight to 42 stores.
- Con: the Washington stores have different weather patterns, so Oregon does not test those.

**Decision: approved.** Oregon expansion on November 4. The full rollout stays on November 18, but
Washington stores get a 3-day dark launch first (forecasts computed and compared, not used for ordering).
