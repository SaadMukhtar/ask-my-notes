# About this sample corpus

Everything in this folder is fiction. "Fernhill Foods" is a made-up grocery-delivery company and the
Platform & Forecasting team is a made-up team. The notes exist so that Ask My Notes has a realistic,
public corpus to index, test, and evaluate against.

A few notes in `inbox/` deliberately contain prompt-injection attempts (text that tries to give
instructions to an AI assistant). They are there to test the guardrails. Do not "fix" them.
